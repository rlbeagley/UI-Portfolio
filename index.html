My Workflow

I am currently beginning my journey as a UI/UX designer!

Throughout this term, I've been learning to bring color, typography, and layout together with intention, using contrast, scale, and negative space to guide the eye and keep things consistent.

I've also been diving into Gestalt principles and usability heuristics, thinking about how people actually perceive and interact with a design, not just how it looks.

More than anything, I've realized how much I love the creative side of this work. I enjoy making each design feel unique, personal, and tailored rather than generic.
